Domestic violence FIR filing rate vs experien

In many states, the FIR filing rate for domestic violence is under 5% of women who report experiencing it.

Policy implication

The gap between experience and FIR filing reveals how far the justice system is from actually protecting women.

Data vintage
This map uses NFHS-5 (2019-21). NFHS-6 (2023-24) state fact sheets were released in May 2026, but they are provisional and report fewer indicators, so the figures here have not been replaced.
Data note
This map sets NFHS-5 survey experience against police-recorded cases. NFHS-6 reports spousal violence overall (map X216), and the FIR comparison has not been redone, so the figures remain as made.
