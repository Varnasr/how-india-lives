Honour killings recorded, NCRB 2024

NCRB recorded 36 murders with honour killing as the motive in 2024. Bihar had the most (6), then Jharkhand and Punjab (5 each). Manipur has the highest rate, 3.07 per crore, from one case. Twenty-three states and territories recorded none.
Policy implication
A zero means no murder was recorded under this motive, which is not the same as none occurring: a family that reports an honour killing as a suicide or an accident leaves no trace in this table. With counts this small the map is a list of where the motive was written down, and it should not be read as a ranking.
