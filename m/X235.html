Unmet need for family planning, NFHS-6

Unmet need for family planning ranges from 4.4 per cent in Andhra Pradesh to 21.0 per cent in Meghalaya. The India figure is 8.5 per cent, down from 9.4 in NFHS-5.
Policy implication
Unmet need is a woman's stated wish to delay or stop childbearing while using no contraception, and it covers currently married women only.
