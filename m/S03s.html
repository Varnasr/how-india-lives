Life expectancy at birth

Life expectancy at birth in Kerala is 77.3 years and in Chhattisgarh 65.2, a gap of 12 years within one country (SRS abridged life tables, 2018-22).

Policy implication

The 12-year life expectancy gap between Indian states exceeds the gap between some developed and developing nations.
