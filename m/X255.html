Services share of state output, MoSPI 2024-25

Services are 89.4% of Chandigarh's output and 86.1% of Delhi's, and 69.2% of Karnataka's. They are 35.5% in Madhya Pradesh, 32.3% in Chhattisgarh and 29.9% in Sikkim. The middle state is at 49.2%.

Policy implication

Services here include public administration and real estate, which includes the imputed rent of owner-occupied homes, so a high share does not mean a state sells software or finance. Mining, electricity and construction are counted outside services.
