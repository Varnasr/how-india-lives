Women self-help groups per lakh people, 2026

Open the full-size image

DAY-NRLM had set up 92.30 lakh women self-help groups by August 2026, 657 per lakh people. Andhra Pradesh (1,549), Meghalaya (1,414), Tripura (1,309), Odisha (1,182) and West Bengal (1,171) have the most per head, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (131), Punjab (185), Haryana (205) and Goa (218) the fewest. The southern states are not all high: Karnataka has 437 and Tamil Nadu 452.
Policy implication
This counts groups and not members, and it is cumulative, so it includes groups that have since stopped meeting. The denominator is the whole population while the Mission works in rural areas, which pulls down heavily urban states and territories. Delhi and Chandigarh are outside the Mission and are grey. Membership by state, which the older map showed, is not in this table.
