Muslim total fertility rate

Muslim TFR has converged toward Hindu TFR in southern states but remains 0.5-1.0 higher in UP and Bihar.

Policy implication

Education and income drive fertility more than theology does. Muslim TFR in Kerala equals Hindu TFR in UP.
