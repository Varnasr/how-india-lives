Women with low BMI, NFHS-6

Open the full-size image

Women with bmi below 18.5 ranges from 3.1 per cent in Andaman and Nicobar to 29.2 per cent in Jharkhand. The India figure is 19.7 per cent, up from 18.7 in NFHS-5.
Policy implication
A BMI below 18.5 marks undernutrition in an adult woman, and it moves slowly between survey rounds.
