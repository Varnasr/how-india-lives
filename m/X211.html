Gender gap in literacy

Rajasthan's literacy gap is 23.2 percentage points, ten times Kerala's 2.2. The 14 states left grey are not included in the published round.
Policy implication
A literacy gap among people aged 7 and above is an accumulated record, not a current one: it holds decades of school enrolment inside a single number, and closing it has a floor set by how long people live.
