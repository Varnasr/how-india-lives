Unemployment rate, PLFS 2025

Unemployment rate ranges from 0.9 per cent in Gujarat to 10.0 per cent in Andaman and Nicobar. The India figure is 3.1 per cent.

Policy implication

A low unemployment rate can mean people cannot afford to stay out of work, so it says little about the quality of jobs.
