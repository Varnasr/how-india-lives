Agricultural mechanisation tractors per 1000

Punjab has 100+ tractors per 1,000 hectares; eastern states have under 10.

Policy implication

Agricultural mechanisation follows landholding size. Marginal farmers can't afford tractors, which deepens the productivity gap.
