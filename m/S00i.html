Share of population over 60 years

Kerala has 16% population over 60; Bihar has 7%. Kerala is ageing at European speeds with Indian incomes.

Policy implication

Ageing states face a care economy crisis. Who looks after the elderly when women enter the workforce?

Data vintage
This map uses the 2011 Census, the latest published count by state and district. Populations and shares will have changed since.
Data note
The Census of India is taken once a decade. The 2011 Census is the latest to publish state and district tables, and the next census is planned for 2027, so there is no later official count to replace these figures. Shares, ratios and populations will have moved since 2011.
