Children with diarrhoea receiving zinc

Only 30% of children with diarrhoea receive zinc treatment nationally, a cheap lifesaver that goes underused.

Policy implication

Zinc with ORS reduces diarrhoea duration by 25%. Cost is not the barrier; awareness and the supply chain are.
