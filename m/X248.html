Vacant judicial posts, subordinate courts, 2026

Nearly one in five sanctioned posts in the subordinate judiciary is vacant (18.8 per cent), from none in Lakshadweep to 47.1 per cent in Ladakh and 42.4 in Meghalaya.

Policy implication

Small territories swing on a few posts: Ladakh's 47.1 per cent is 8 of 17 posts. The source's own national vacancy total (4,868) does not match its state rows (4,880), so the India figure here is computed from the rows.
