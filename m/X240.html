Crimes against women recorded, NCRB 2024

Registered crimes against women run from 5.1 per lakh women in Nagaland to 130.7 in Delhi. The India figure is 64.6, from 66.2 in 2023.
Policy implication
This counts registrations, not incidence. A higher figure can reflect easier reporting as well as more crime, so states should not be ranked on it.
