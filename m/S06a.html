Non-Hindu population share

In Mizoram, Meghalaya, and Nagaland, over 85% of the population is non-Hindu. India's religious map is not monolithic.

Policy implication

Religious composition determines school systems, family law, dietary norms, and festival holidays at the state level.

Data note
The Census of India is taken once a decade. The 2011 Census is the latest to publish state and district tables, and the next census is planned for 2027, so there is no later official count to replace these figures. Shares, ratios and populations will have moved since 2011.
