High blood pressure in men, NFHS-6

Men with elevated blood pressure ranges from 14.1 per cent in Uttar Pradesh to 36.6 per cent in Kerala. The India figure is 22.1 per cent, down from 24.0 in NFHS-5.

Policy implication

The figure includes people already on medicine, so a high share can reflect disease, screening, or both.
