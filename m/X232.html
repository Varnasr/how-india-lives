Women with their own mobile phone, NFHS-6

Women with a mobile phone they use ranges from 47.8 per cent in Chhattisgarh to 91.6 per cent in Sikkim. The India figure is 63.6 per cent, up from 53.9 in NFHS-5.
Policy implication
Having a phone is not the same as having a data connection or using it freely.
