Anaemia in women 15-49

Over 65% of women in Jharkhand and Bihar are anaemic vs 33% in Manipur.

Policy implication

Anaemia saps productivity, worsens maternal mortality, and passes disadvantage to the next generation.

This map uses NFHS-5 (2019-21). NFHS-6 (2023-24) state fact sheets were released in May 2026, but they are provisional and report fewer indicators, so the figures here have not been replaced.
Data note
NFHS-6 (2023-24) state fact sheets carry 101 indicators against 131 in NFHS-5 and do not repeat this measure, so NFHS-5 (2019-21) remains the latest state-level figure.
