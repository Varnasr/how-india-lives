Christian population share

Christians form 87% of Nagaland's population but only 2.3% nationally, an extreme geographic concentration.

Policy implication

Christian minority institutions (schools, hospitals) are the primary service providers in NE states, far from a marginal presence.

Data note
The Census of India is taken once a decade. The 2011 Census is the latest to publish state and district tables, and the next census is planned for 2027, so there is no later official count to replace these figures. Shares, ratios and populations will have moved since 2011.
