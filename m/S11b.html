MGNREGA women as % of total person-days

Women constitute 60%+ of MGNREGA person-days in Tamil Nadu and Kerala but under 20% in UP.

Policy implication

MGNREGA is India's largest women's employment programme where women participate; enabling women's access elsewhere is a policy win.
