Hindi vs English newspaper readership ratio

Hindi newspapers outsell English ones 10:1 in UP; in Kerala, English dominates. The two media ecosystems are as different as two countries.

Policy implication

Language-specific misinformation travels through language-specific media; fact-checking must be multilingual.
