Marginal farmers holdings under 1 hectare

Open the full-size image

In Bihar and Bengal, 80%+ of farm holdings are under 1 hectare, marginal farmers with marginal income.
Policy implication
Marginal farmers cannot mechanise, diversify, or access formal credit. Farm policy designed for Punjab doesn't work here.
