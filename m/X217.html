Women married before 18, NFHS-6

Child marriage fell nationally from 23.3 to 20.1 per cent, but it is still 36.4 per cent in West Bengal, more than twelve times the 2.9 per cent of Kerala.

Policy implication

Andaman and Nicobar and Tamil Nadu have no published figure in the fact sheets, so they are grey. A grey state here has no figure, not a low one.
