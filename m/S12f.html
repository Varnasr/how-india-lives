Atlas / Reproductive choices

Vasectomy rate among married men

Vasectomy rates are below 1% nationally. India's family planning programme never invested in male methods.

Policy implication

The near-zero vasectomy rate is a policy failure rather than a biological inevitability. It reflects gendered programme design.

Data vintage
This map uses NFHS-5 (2019-21). NFHS-6 (2023-24) state fact sheets were released in May 2026, but they are provisional and report fewer indicators, so the figures here have not been replaced.
Data note
NFHS-6 reports male sterilisation only within the contraceptive method mix of currently married women, which is not this map's measure, so NFHS-5 (2019-21) remains the latest state-level figure for it.
