Underweight children, NFHS-6

Open the full-size image

Children under 5 who are underweight ranges from 11.2 per cent in Sikkim to 41.3 per cent in Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. The India figure is 31.8 per cent, down from 32.1 in NFHS-5.
Policy implication
Underweight mixes stunting and wasting, so it is a rougher guide than either measure alone.
