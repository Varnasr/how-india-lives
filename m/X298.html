Rural households with tap water supply, July 2026

Open the full-size image

By 18 July 2026 states reported tap water supply to 15.89 crore of 19.36 crore rural households, 82.1 per cent against 16.7 per cent when the Jal Jeevan Mission began in August 2019. Ten states and territories report 100 per cent, among them Telangana, Punjab, Haryana and Himachal Pradesh. Kerala (55.1), Jharkhand (55.4), West Bengal (56.8) and Rajasthan (59.1) are lowest, followed by Madhya Pradesh (74.9) and Andhra Pradesh (75.5).
Policy implication
This counts household connections reported by the states in the Mission's own system, not water that runs or water that is safe. The Ministry's functionality assessments are separate and not in this table. Kerala's low figure is as reported and the answer does not explain it. Delhi and Chandigarh have no rural population and are grey.
