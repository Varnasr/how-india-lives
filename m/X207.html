Urban consumption per person

Urban spending per person is higher than rural spending in all 36 states and territories. The national gap is ₹6,996 against ₹4,122, about 70 per cent.
Policy implication
Urban-rural is the axis most Indian transfer programmes are least able to see: entitlements are settled at the village or ward, while the gap that drives migration sits between them.
