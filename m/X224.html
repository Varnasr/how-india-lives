Overweight women, NFHS-6

Women who are overweight or obese ranges from 19.6 per cent in Assam to 51.3 per cent in Puducherry. The India figure is 30.7 per cent, up from 24.0 in NFHS-5.

Policy implication

Overweight now sits beside undernutrition in many states, so one national health message misses both.
