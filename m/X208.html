Rural consumption inequality

Kerala has the seventh highest rural spending per person of 36 states and territories and the second most unequal rural distribution, at a Gini of 0.255 against 0.237 nationally. A high average is not a flat one.

Policy implication

Ranking states by mean consumption and ranking them by its dispersion produce different lists. Any transfer designed off the mean alone is targeting a state that does not exist.
