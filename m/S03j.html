Neonatal mortality rate first 28 days

Neonatal mortality (first 28 days) accounts for 60%+ of infant deaths, most of them in the first 48 hours.

Policy implication

Neonatal deaths cluster around birth; facility-based newborn care units in district hospitals save the most lives per rupee.

Data note
