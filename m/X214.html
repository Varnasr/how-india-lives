Women who have used the internet, NFHS-6

The share of women who have ever used the internet nearly doubled between the two rounds, from 33.3 to 64.3 per cent. It still runs from 48.8 per cent in Tripura to 94.0 per cent in Goa.
Policy implication
Ever having used the internet is a low bar. It says nothing about owning a phone, reading confidently online, or using a service independently, so it should not be read as digital inclusion.
