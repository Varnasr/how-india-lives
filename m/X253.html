Agriculture share of state output, MoSPI 2024-25

Agriculture is 41.3% of Madhya Pradesh's output and 38.0% of Tripura's, against 0.3% in Delhi and 0.5% in Chandigarh. The middle state is at 18.1%. Manipur's share fell most between 2022-23 and 2024-25, by 3.4 points.

Policy implication

A share of output is not a share of jobs, and farm work employs a much larger share of workers than its share of output suggests. The share also falls when other sectors grow faster, so a lower number is not proof that farming did badly.
