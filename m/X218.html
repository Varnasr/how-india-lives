Child wasting, NFHS-6

Children under 5 who are wasted ranges from 8.1 per cent in Mizoram to 24.2 per cent in Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. The India figure is 19.0 per cent, down from 19.3 in NFHS-5.

Policy implication

Wasting reflects recent shortfalls in food and bouts of illness, so it can move faster than stunting. One survey year is not a trend.
