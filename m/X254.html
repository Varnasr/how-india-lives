Manufacturing share of state output, MoSPI 2024-25

Open the full-size image

Manufacturing is 38.4% of Sikkim's output, 32.8% of Gujarat's and 27.8% of Goa's, but under 2% in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur and Mizoram. The middle state is at 11.4%. Gujarat's share fell 2.1 points in two years and Sikkim's rose 2.7.
Policy implication
In a small economy one large unit can move the share a long way, so Sikkim's figure and the small shares in the north-east and the islands say as much about size as about policy.
