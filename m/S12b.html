Condom use by currently married couples

Condom use by married couples is under 10% in most states. Male methods remain marginalised.

Policy implication

Condom promotion has failed nationally; the contraceptive burden remains almost entirely on women.

Data note
NFHS-6 (2023-24) state fact sheets carry 101 indicators against 131 in NFHS-5 and do not repeat this measure, so NFHS-5 (2019-21) remains the latest state-level figure.
