Secondary school dropout, UDISE+ 2024-25

Secondary school dropout rate ranges from 2.0 per cent in Chandigarh to 20.0 per cent in West Bengal.

Policy implication

Bihar is grey because the API reports exactly 0, which is not a credible measurement. A grey state here has no usable figure, not a low one.
