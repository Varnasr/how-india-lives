Youth unemployment rate, PLFS 2025

Youth unemployment rate ranges from 2.5 per cent in Gujarat to 31.2 per cent in Andaman and Nicobar. The India figure is 9.9 per cent.
Policy implication
Youth rates run well above adult rates, and small states rest on small samples, so single figures are noisy.
