Birth registration children under 5

Birth registration for children under 5 exceeds 95% in southern states but is under 60% in Assam and Nagaland.

Policy implication

Unregistered children have no legal identity. They can't access school admission, welfare schemes, or age-proof documentation.

Data note
The Census of India is taken once a decade. The 2011 Census is the latest to publish state and district tables, and the next census is planned for 2027, so there is no later official count to replace these figures. Shares, ratios and populations will have moved since 2011.
