Tobacco use among women, NFHS-6

Women who use tobacco ranges from 0.5 per cent in Chandigarh to 61.0 per cent in Mizoram. The India figure is 8.4 per cent, down from 8.9 in NFHS-5.

Policy implication

This is self-reported, and willingness to report differs by state and by sex.
