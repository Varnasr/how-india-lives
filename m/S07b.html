English speakers % who speak English as 1st

In Nagaland and Meghalaya, 30%+ speak English as their first or second language; in Bihar, under 1%.

Policy implication

English proficiency maps onto economic opportunity in the service sector, which gives NE states an underrecognised advantage.

Data note
The Census of India is taken once a decade. The 2011 Census is the latest to publish state and district tables, and the next census is planned for 2027, so there is no later official count to replace these figures. Shares, ratios and populations will have moved since 2011.
