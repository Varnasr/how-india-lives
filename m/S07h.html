Out-of-school children 6-14 years

Out-of-school children (6-14) cluster in UP, Bihar, and Rajasthan, driven by poverty, child labour, and distance.

Policy implication

The last-mile children are hardest to reach: migrant families, disabled children, and girls in rural areas.
